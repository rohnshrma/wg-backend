## Priority order (highest-value, most realistic first)

Local, topically-relevant, low-competition links beat generic high-authority ones for a local business — a single relevant local link usually outweighs a directory listing.

### Tier 1 — Local business/education citations (fast, high completion rate)

| Target | Why | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Covered separately in `GBP_CONTENT_PACK.md` | — |
| Justdial | Already appeared in the original audit's competitor research (competitors are listed there) | Claim/verify listing, ensure NAP matches the website exactly |
| Sulekha | Same — competitors are listed there for "Python Training Gurgaon" | Claim/verify listing |
| IndiaMART | Common for training-institute listings in India | Create/verify listing |
| Yellow Pages India / local Gurugram business directories | Standard local citation building | Create/verify listing |

**NAP consistency matters more than the number of listings** — every one of these needs the exact same address format as the website (`M-18, Ground Floor, Old DLF Colony, Sector-14, Gurugram, Haryana`) and the exact same phone number. Inconsistent NAP across directories actively confuses Google's local relevance signals rather than helping them.

## What "success" looks like here, honestly

Local citation building (Tier 1) can realistically move in weeks. Educational partnerships and press (Tiers 2 and 4) realistically take months and a real relationship, not a template email. Don't let the content calendar and location-page work in `GROWTH_BACKLOG.md` stall waiting on backlinks — they compound in parallel, not in sequence.
