## Review acquisition workflow

1. **Trigger point:** immediately after a student completes their final project/milestone (highest satisfaction moment, per the course FAQs' own "milestone project" structure) — not at random or only at placement (that excludes everyone who hasn't been placed yet, undercounting real satisfied students).
2. **Ask method:** a direct WhatsApp message (already a core contact channel per `siteConfig.contact.whatsapp`) with a one-tap Google review link — not email, which has much lower completion rates for this kind of ask.
3. **Never incentivize reviews with discounts/free content** — against Google's review policies and risks the whole profile.
4. **Respond to every review, positive or negative,** within a few days — response rate itself is a trust/ranking signal, not just PR.
